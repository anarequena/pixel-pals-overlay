Drop your own lofi .mp3 / .wav / .ogg / .m4a files in this folder.

They appear automatically in the overlay's music player (use the ⏭ button to
cycle between the built-in lofi loop and your tracks). No restart needed for the
list to refresh the next time the app starts; use the tray menu "Open music
folder" for quick access.

Tip: search for "royalty free lofi" / "creative commons lofi" to find tracks you
can legally use.
